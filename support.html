Common questions

How do I leave a run?

The button with three bars, top left, or Escape on a keyboard. Your run is saved: the menu then offers RESUME RUN.

The music is too loud.

Settings, Audio tab. Music and sound have their own sliders, and the top strip shows what is playing.

Can I turn off the glow?

Yes. Settings, Video tab: NEON GLOW for the halos and SCANLINES for the CRT grain.

How do I change the snake?

Settings, Game tab, or Tab on a keyboard while playing. New programs unlock at sectors 4, 8 and 12.

Requirements

iOS 15 or later, or macOS 11 or later. The game runs entirely offline.
