Support

Something not working, or an idea? Write to javier.bascones@gmail.com — you will get an answer from the person who made it, usually within a couple of days.

How do I steer?

Arrow keys or WASD on a keyboard. On a touch screen, hold to one side of the cycle and it turns that way. The turn is queued and lands at the centre of the next cell, so you can press early.

What kills me?

The data walls, the firewall gates when they are closed, the sentries patrolling their lane, the edge of the grid — and your own wall of light, which is the one that gets you.

What are the upgrades?

  • Brake — hold to glide at half speed.
  • Shield — survives the first crash of each phase.
  • Boost — hold to run fast; it eats the meter.
  • Scan — marks the nearest disc across the grid.

The backdrop distracts me

Settings → Video → Backdrop: off, sharp, soft or blurred.

Does it need internet?

No. It has no networking code, no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

Requirements

iOS 15 or later, macOS 11 or later, or Windows and Android.
