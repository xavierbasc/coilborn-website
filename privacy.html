Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Coilborn collects no personal data at all. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

What is stored, and where

The app keeps your settings (music and sound volume) and your child's progress in its own private storage on the device. It never leaves the device, it is not backed up to any server of ours, and it is removed when the app is deleted.

Network

The app ships without any networking code: it works fully offline and never contacts a server.

Children

Coilborn is made for young children and follows Apple's Kids Category rules: no behavioural advertising, no data collection, and anything that leaves the game — settings, quitting — sits behind a control that has to be held down for a couple of seconds.

Purchases

The app is sold on the App Store for a single payment. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.
